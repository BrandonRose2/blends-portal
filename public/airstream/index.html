How to use this portal

Everything for turning the Airstream into an Airbnb, in one place. Works on a phone or a computer, no account needed.

The four sections

Use the menu at the top, or these direct links (you can bookmark or text them):

  • Overview
    What's already in the Airstream and the next steps.
  • Layouts
    Two floor plan options for guests, using what's already built. View only.
  • Guest setup
    Style directions, what guests need, and an Airbnb listing checklist. View only.
  • Airstream info
    The form to fill out: measurements, hookups, bathroom, guests, and listings you like.

Filling out the form

  1. Open Airstream info and enter the access code Brandon sent you. Your device remembers it.
  2. Fill in what you know. Nothing is required.
  3. Listings you like: paste a link to any Airbnb or photo you like, tick what you like about it, and add a note. Tap + Add another for more.
  4. Tap Save at the bottom. Come back anytime to edit and save again.

Other buttons

  • View as text shows a summary you can Copy into a text or email.

Photos

The form can't upload pictures. Text Brandon photos and a short video of the inside, the outside, and where it's parked.

If something goes wrong

  • Code didn't work: check capitals and the dash, or ask Brandon again.
  • Didn't save: check your connection and tap Save again.

Layouts

Two ways to set it up for guests

Top-down, not to scale until we have measurements. Both reuse the oak counter (as the kitchenette) and the bench (as a sofa or daybed).

A · Rear bedroom

  • Bedroom feels private at the back, away from the door
  • Existing rear TV and mirror wall becomes the headboard wall
  • Shampoo bowl swaps out for a kitchen sink on the same plumbing
  • Sleeps 2, or 3 with the daybed

B · Front bedroom, rear lounge

  • Rear windows and TV wall become a bright lounge, the hero photo for the listing
  • Bed tucked in the front curve, cozy "sleeping nook" feel
  • More open floor when you walk in
  • Sleeps 2

The big question: bathroom

Guests will expect one. Options: a compact wet bath inside (toilet + shower in one small room, needs water, a tank or sewer, and a water heater), a composting toilet plus an outdoor shower, or access to a bathroom nearby on the property. This choice decides the final layout, so it's in the form.

Guest setup

Style, comfort and the listing

Pick a style direction

1 · Polished modern

  • White bedding, black fixtures
  • Show off the aluminum outside
  • Clean, hotel-like

2 · Warm minimal

  • Linen, oak, plants
  • Warm 2700–3000K light
  • Calm cabin feel

3 · Retro road trip

  • Vintage colors and patterns
  • Record player, camp mugs
  • Fun, very photogenic

What guests need

  • Real mattress (not foam pad), blackout shades
  • AC and a small heater (already has AC)
  • Wi-Fi, outlets + USB by the bed
  • Mini fridge, kettle or coffee maker, basic dishes
  • Hooks, a luggage spot, a full-length mirror
  • Outdoor seating, string lights, a mat at the door
  • Smoke + CO detector, fire extinguisher, first-aid kit
  • Smart lock or lockbox for self check-in

Airbnb listing checklist

  • Check short-term rental rules and permits for the parking spot
  • Rental insurance that covers guests
  • Bright daytime photos: outside first, then bed, kitchenette, bath, outdoor area
  • Title that sells the experience, e.g. "Restored Airstream with patio"
  • House rules, check-in guide, Wi-Fi card inside
  • Cleaning plan and fee between guests
  • Price check against nearby tiny homes and trailers
