Airstream studio

Concepts for the barber studio

Built around what's already in the trailer: the oak counter with the shampoo bowl, the built-in bench, and the rear vanity with the mirror and TV. Top-down plans, not to scale until we have measurements.

A · Rear station

  • Uses the existing mirror, TV and vanity as the station
  • Private-suite feel: the client walks past everything to the chair
  • Washes are a short walk to the bowl

B · Center chair

  • Chair sits right beside the shampoo bowl, so washes are quick
  • Tools on the oak counter within arm's reach
  • Rear becomes a neon sign and product wall, a backdrop for cut photos

Station wall (Layout A)

  1. TV: loop the booking QR code, socials and cut photos
  2. Backlit mirror: LED strip behind it, 3000–3500K, high-CRI so fades look true
  3. Window: frosted film for privacy, still lets light in
  4. Outlet strip at counter height for clippers + charging dock
  5. Tool rail with magnetic holsters on the side wall
  6. Drawers: sterilizer jar, capes, towels, backstock
  7. Speakers: already installed

Before opening, check California Board of Barbering & Cosmetology rules for mobile or trailer shops, and local zoning, power and water hookups.

Pages

  • Home: everything at left, one scroll
  • Barber pages: one per barber with bio, work photos and their Book button (helps people find each barber by name on Google)
  • Services: menu with prices
  • Location: map, hours, parking, directions
  • FAQ: walk-ins, payment, cancellations

Booking

Each barber's button goes straight to their own Booksy link, so no new booking system is needed. Booksy stays the calendar.

Google / SEO plan

  • Google Business Profile updated with the new address on moving day
  • Name, address and phone written exactly the same on the site, Google, Booksy, Yelp and Instagram
  • "BarberShop" business info built into the site so Google reads hours, location and prices
  • Page titles like "Blends Barbershop | Barber in Los Angeles"
  • A page for each barber, so their names are searchable
  • Real photos with descriptions (no stock photos)
  • Fast load on phones; works without the Booksy app
  • Ask happy clients for Google reviews with a QR code on the TV and mirror
