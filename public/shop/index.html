How to use this portal

This portal has everything for the new Blends Barbershop website in one place. It works on a phone or a computer. You don't need an account or an app.

The three sections

Use the menu at the top, or go straight to a section with these links. You can bookmark any of them or text them to someone.

  • Overview
    Quick summary of the project and the next steps.
  • Website plan
    What the homepage will look like, the pages, how each barber's Book button works, and the plan for showing up on Google. View only.
  • Shop info
    The form to fill out. This is the only part where you type anything.

Filling out the shop info form

  1. Open Shop info. The first time, it asks for the access code Brandon sent you. Type it and tap Open. Your phone or computer remembers it after that.
  2. Fill in what you know and skip anything you don't. Nothing is required.
  3. Barbers: one box per barber. Tap + Add another barber for more, or Remove to delete one. The Booking link is that barber's personal Booksy page, and it's what their Book button on the website will open.
  4. Services: name, price and time for each service. Tap + Add a service for more rows. You can skip this if you'd rather just link to Booksy.
  5. Websites you like: copy a link from your browser and paste it in, tick what you like about it, and write a quick note. Add as many as you want. This is the most helpful part for making the site feel like you.
  6. Tap Save at the bottom. The save bar stays at the bottom of the screen while you scroll. You'll see "Saved" with the time.

Coming back to edit

Everything you save stays here. Open the same link anytime, change anything, and tap Save again. Brandon sees your latest version. If you try to leave with unsaved changes, your browser warns you first.

Other buttons

  • View as text shows everything you entered as a clean summary. Tap Copy to paste it into a text or email.
  • How to use this portal (top of the page) opens this guide again.

Photos and logo

The form can't upload pictures. Text or email your logo, haircut photos, photos of each barber and photos of the new shop to Brandon directly.

If something goes wrong

  • "That code didn't work": check for typos (capital letters and the dash count), or ask Brandon for the code again.
  • "Didn't save": check your internet connection and tap Save again. Don't close the page until it says Saved.
  • Page looks old: refresh the page.

Booking

Each barber's button goes straight to their own Booksy link, so no new booking system is needed. Booksy stays the calendar.

Google / SEO plan

  • Google Business Profile moved to the new Encino address on moving day (old address marked as moved, not deleted)
  • Name, address and phone written exactly the same on the site, Google, Booksy, Yelp and Instagram
  • "BarberShop" business info built into the site so Google reads hours, location and prices
  • Page titles like "Blends Barbershop | Barber in Encino"
  • A page for each barber, so their names are searchable
  • Real photos with descriptions (no stock photos)
  • Fast load on phones; works without the Booksy app
  • Ask happy clients for Google reviews with a QR code on the TV and mirror
